# Methodology

This document outlines the methodology followed in the study titled:

Rivera Capacho, E. E., Bossa, C. P. D., Campos, M. del C., Rincon-Yanez, D., Rangel-Navia, H., & Bianchini, E. M. G. (2025). **Telemedicine-supported structured Orofacial Myofunctional Therapy model for Obstructive Sleep Apnea: Patients’ report outcomes measurements**. Respiratory Medicine, 249, 108460. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rmed.2025.108460

---

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective observational cohort study
- **Sample Size**: 87 adult patients
- **Period**: November 2021 to November 2022
- **Setting**: NEUMOMED Sleep and Pulmonology Clinic, Medellín, Colombia
- **Ethical Approval**: University of Pamplona’s Ethics and Environmental Impact Committee (Minutes No. 007, Dec 16, 2020)

---

## Inclusion Criteria

- Adults aged over 18 with a diagnosis of:
  - Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) or
  - Primary Snoring (PS)
- Treated using the Smart Therapy Manager® system
- Minimum of 8 weeks of Orofacial Myofunctional Therapy (OMT)
- Completed pre- and post-treatment assessments

---

## Exclusion Criteria

- Incomplete clinical records
- Under 18 years of age
- Pregnant women
- Cognitive limitations preventing therapy adherence

---

## Intervention

- **Therapy Protocol**: ST-OSA-PS-OMT model
- **Delivery Mode**: Remote AI-enhanced platform via Smart Therapy Manager®
- **Therapy Duration**: Median of 12.5 weeks
- **Assessments**:
  - Pre/post Epworth Sleepiness Scale (ESS)
  - Pre/post PSG or PLG studies
  - AI-assisted PROMs survey: EPE-TMO-TRS-PROMs
  - Clinical phenotype evaluation

---

## Outcome Variables

- Subjective sleepiness (ESS)
- AHI, SpO2, and snoring index
- Clinical observations (e.g., palate, uvula, orofacial muscle tone)
- PROMs feedback (treatment satisfaction, adherence perception)

---

## Data Capture

- All data was extracted from electronic clinical records
- Anonymization was performed to protect patient privacy
- Data was structured into a tabular format for open publication

---

## Tools Used

- Smart Therapy Manager® software
- Epworth Sleepiness Scale
- Type I and III polysomnography
- EPE-TMO-TRS-PROMs (internal validated scale)

---

This methodology ensures the dataset adheres to clinical research standards and supports reproducibility in sleep medicine and digital therapeutics research.
