# Study Summary

**Title**: [Telemedicine-supported structured Orofacial Myofunctional Therapy model for Obstructive Sleep Apnea: Patients' report outcomes measurements](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rmed.2025.108460)

---

## Objective

To evaluate the effectiveness of AI-enhanced telemedicine delivery of Orofacial Myofunctional Therapy (OMT) in improving clinical outcomes for adult patients with Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) and Primary Snoring (PS).

---

## Study Design

- **Type**: Retrospective observational cohort study
- **Sample Size**: 87 adult patients
- **Setting**: NEUMOMED Sleep and Pulmonology Clinic, Medellín, Colombia
- **Therapy Platform**: Smart Therapy Manager®
- **Duration**: Median of 12.5 weeks of OMT
- **Ethical Approval**: University of Pamplona’s Ethics and Environmental Impact Committee

---

## Methods

Participants received personalized ST-OSA-PS-OMT therapy guided by AI through a digital platform. Clinical data was collected pre- and post-treatment using:

- Epworth Sleepiness Scale (ESS)
- Polysomnography (PSG) or Limited Polygraphy (PLG)
- PROMs (EPE-TMO-TRS-PROMs)
- Orofacial muscle function assessments

---

## Key Findings

- Significant reduction in daytime sleepiness (measured via ESS)
- Clinical improvements in AHI and oxygen saturation for a subgroup
- Positive subjective feedback via PROMs, especially in:
  - Adherence
  - Ease of use
  - Motivation
- Best outcomes observed in patients with specific oral-facial phenotypes

---

## Implications

This study demonstrates the potential for AI-assisted telehealth delivery of OMT to improve access and outcomes for patients with OSA/PS. The dataset supports further exploration into phenotype-based personalization of therapy.
