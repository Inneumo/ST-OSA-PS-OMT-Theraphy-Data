# Ethical Considerations

This dataset was generated in the context of a clinical study conducted in accordance with ethical standards for research involving human subjects.

---

## Ethical Approval

The study protocol was reviewed and approved by the **Ethics and Environmental Impact Committee of the University of Pamplona**, under:

- **Approval Number**: Minutes No. 007
- **Approval Date**: December 16, 2020

---

## Informed Consent

- All participants included in the study provided **written informed consent**.
- Participants consented to the use of their anonymized clinical data for research and educational purposes.

---

## Data Anonymization

- Personal identifiers such as names, contact details, and national IDs were removed.
- Indirect identifiers were assessed and masked when necessary to ensure non-traceability.
- The dataset was manually reviewed to confirm full anonymization prior to publication.

---

## Data Handling and Storage

- Patient records were stored securely at NEUMOMED Sleep and Pulmonology Clinic.
- Only authorized clinical and research personnel had access to the raw data.
- Data was exported, cleaned, and structured in a secure offline environment before open release.

---

## Compliance

This study complies with:
- The Declaration of Helsinki
- Colombian national laws for biomedical research
- Institutional data protection policies

---

For questions or concerns regarding data privacy or ethical practices, please open an issue at the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Inneumo/ST-OSA-PS-OMT-Therapy-Data/issues).
